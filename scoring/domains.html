Motor Dimensions A through E Specification

The GMFM assessment is structured across five developmental motor dimensions. Each dimension isolates specific postural control mechanisms, balance transitions, and locomotor competencies.

Document Contents

  • Dimension B: Sitting (20 Items)
  • Dimension C: Crawling & Kneeling (14 Items)
  • Dimension D: Standing (13 Items)
  • Dimension E: Walking, Running & Jumping (24 Items)

Dimension A: Lying & Rolling (17 Items)

Focuses on primitive postural reflexes, head control, supine/prone positioning, and axial rotational rolling. Key clinical evaluation criteria include:

  • Head elevation and midline alignment in supine and prone positions.
  • Active weight-bearing through forearms and extended arms in prone.
  • Segmental and log rolling from supine to prone and prone to supine over left and right sides.
  • Reaching across midline with upper extremities in supine.

Dimension B: Sitting (20 Items)

Evaluates static and dynamic seated equilibrium, trunk righting reactions, and transitions between floor and sitting:

  • Supported and unsupported sitting balance on mat with erect spine for 3 to 10 seconds.
  • Reaching forward and laterally for toys without loss of balance.
  • Transitional movement: pivot in sitting, lowering to prone, and moving from prone into sitting.
  • Bench sitting with feet supported on floor and reaching toward floor.

Dimension C: Crawling & Kneeling (14 Items)

Isolates quadripedal pelvic stabilization, reciprocal extremity coordination, and upright kneeling:

  • Maintaining four-point quadruped positioning for 10 seconds.
  • Reciprocal crawling on hands and knees forward for 1.8 meters (6 feet).
  • Attaining high-kneeling and half-kneeling with and without upper extremity support.
  • Transitioning from half-kneeling to standing.

Dimension D: Standing (13 Items)

Measures anti-gravity extensor tone, vertical postural alignment, and bipedal balance:

  • Pull-to-stand through bench or rail with bilateral and unilateral lower extremity dominance.
  • Free standing without hand support for 3 to 20 seconds.
  • Single-leg standing balance on right and left lower extremities.
  • Controlled lowering from standing down to the floor without falling.

Dimension E: Walking, Running & Jumping (24 Items)

The most demanding functional dimension, evaluating forward ambulation, dynamic agility, and vertical propulsion:

  • Cruising along furniture and walking forward holding one or two hands.
  • Independent bipedal walking forward for 10 consecutive steps with reciprocal arm swing.
  • Pivot turning 180 degrees while walking without stopping.
  • Walking backwards, stepping over obstacles, stair ascent and descent with alternating feet.
  • Running 4.5 meters (15 feet), stopping cleanly, and bilateral standing broad jumps.
