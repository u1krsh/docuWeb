Gross Motor Function Measure (GMFM) System Overview

Clinical Instrument Background
The Gross Motor Function Measure (GMFM) is an evaluative clinical tool developed to assess changes in gross motor function over time in children with cerebral palsy (CP) and Down syndrome. MotorMeasure Pro implements the full GMFM-88 scale with complete item catalogs, validation boundaries, and domain aggregations.

1. The Five Gross Motor Dimensions

The GMFM-88 assesses 88 distinct motor tasks categorized into five developmental motor dimensions:

Dimension Clinical Description Item Range Total Items Max Possible Score
Dimension A Lying & Rolling Items 1 – 17 17 items 51 points
Dimension B Sitting Items 18 – 37 20 items 60 points
Dimension C Crawling & Kneeling Items 38 – 51 14 items 42 points
Dimension D Standing Items 52 – 64 13 items 39 points
Dimension E Walking, Running & Jumping Items 65 – 88 24 items 72 points
Total Full GMFM Assessment Items 1 – 88 88 items 264 points

2. Standardized 4-Point Rating Scale

Each item in the GMFM is scored on a standardized 4-point ordinal scale according to how much of the activity the child accomplishes:

Score Standardized Meaning Clinical Criteria & Execution Guidance
0 Does Not Initiate The child is unable to initiate the movement, does not attempt the activity, or initiates less than 10% of the movement range.
1 Initiates Task The child initiates the task, completing greater than 0% but less than 10% of the movement range.
2 Partially Completes The child partially completes the activity (10% to less than 100% of the specified movement criteria or time hold).
3 Completes Independently The child accomplishes 100% of the task criteria independently, achieving full range, balance, and prescribed time.
NT Not Tested / Omitted The item was omitted due to environmental barriers, child fatigue, lack of cooperation, or clinical judgement. Handled specifically by the scoring engine.

3. GMFM-88 vs GMFM-66 Scales

MotorMeasure Pro supports both scale definitions within its data models:

  • GMFM-88: The original comprehensive criterion-referenced measure. Useful for children with severe motor impairments (GMFCS Level IV & V) or children assessed with assistive orthoses/shoes.
  • GMFM-66: An interval-scaled measure derived through Rasch analysis, consisting of a 66-item subset of the original 88 items. Items in MotorMeasure Pro's catalog are tagged with the boolean flag gmfm66: true.
