Visual Theming & Color Tokens

Design Token System
Visual tokens are centralized in src/gmfm_app/theme.py. Views consume color tokens dynamically via get_colors(is_dark: bool), ensuring consistent branding and full dark mode support.

2. Domain Color Coding System

To reduce cognitive load during multi-item testing, each motor dimension is assigned a distinct functional color and Material icon:

DimensionDomain TitleColor HexMaterial Icon
Dimension A Lying & Rolling #EF4444 hotel
Dimension B Sitting #F59E0B weekend
Dimension C Crawling & Kneeling #10B981 child_care
Dimension D Standing #3B82F6 accessibility_new
Dimension E Walking & Running #8B5CF6 directions_run

3. Dark Mode & Contrast Adaptation

MotorMeasure Pro provides complete light and dark themes. The active preference is stored in page.client_storage and restored on launch:

  • Dark Background (DARK_BG): #0F172A (Slate 900). Reduces glare in dimmed clinical treatment rooms.
  • Light Background (LIGHT_BG): #F8FAFC (Slate 50). Optimized for brightly lit therapy centers.
